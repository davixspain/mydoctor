## A note on this codebase

This started as a private hack to manage my own health case. It was
never originally meant to be open-sourced. What you see here is the
result of going back, anonymizing the personal data, generalizing the
prompts, and writing the documentation that I wish had existed when I
started.

If you find bugs, have ideas, or want to talk about the design choices,
open an issue or send me a message. I read everything.
